1Dewpoint — workflows, editor, library, AI, runs, approvals, settings

Context. Brand pass on top of the repo's tokens (tokens.css): a navy rail carries the Dewpoint mark — two circles meeting, vapour becoming a drop (Mist teal + Juniper green) — plus wordmark and "for Juniper Mist". Type: Schibsted Grotesk for wordmark and headings, Instrument Sans for UI, JetBrains Mono for keys, IDs and CEL. Radius 8px, no gradients or glow. Rail follows the spec §10.2 (Home, Workflows, Runs, Agents, Approvals, Connections → folded into Settings tabs as requested). The four uploaded boards were used as inspiration only: kept the canvas grammar (＋ on edges, drawer Setup/Options/Test, pills), replaced their violet "simulated" with neutral slate per spec.

Assumptions. 1440×900. Hero workflow open in the editor: AP offline → AI agent (MCP) → Slack. AI assistant is a side panel; its proposals render as dashed ghost nodes with per-step accept. Run detail shows a failed Slack step paired with "Explain this failure". Both themes via the Theme tweak.

Reasoning. Colour only for meaning (live orange, failed red, verified green, conditional amber); AI features use plain verbs; every destructive/live action is labelled and confirmed. Current production screens are reproduced at the bottom (0a, 0b) for reference.
